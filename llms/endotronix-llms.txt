# Endotronix

> Endotronix, Inc. is a Naperville, Illinois medical device and digital health company
> founded in 2007 and acquired by Edwards Lifesciences in July 2024, where it operates as a
> wholly owned subsidiary. It builds the Cordella Heart Failure System — an implantable
> wireless pulmonary artery (PA) pressure sensor (FDA premarket approval June 2024), the
> myCordella patient tablet application with connected home vitals peripherals, and the
> myCordella Patient Management Portal used by clinicians to review transmitted pressure,
> vitals and symptom data. Endotronix operates no public developer program: there is no
> developer portal, API reference, SDK, webhook catalog, or machine-readable specification
> on any Endotronix host.

## API surface

- https://endotronix.com/llms.txt — 404
- https://endotronix.com/.well-known/agent-card.json — 404
- https://endotronix.com/.well-known/agent.json — 404
- api.endotronix.com, developer.endotronix.com, data.endotronix.com — do not resolve (NXDOMAIN)
- docs.endotronix.com — 302 to an internal Google Workspace Drive

Endotronix / Edwards Lifesciences; no contract or reference is published publicly.
